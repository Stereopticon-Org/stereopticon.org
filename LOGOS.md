# Logos and names

The Stereopticon and Stereo Loom logos, and the names Stereopticon, wiz3D and Stereo Loom,
identify these projects. They are **not** covered by the MIT licence for this site's code
or the CC BY 4.0 licence for the game data. All rights in the logos are reserved.

Official files are in [`brand/`](brand/): SVG and PNG, for dark and light backgrounds.

## You can

- Use the names and unmodified logos to refer to the projects, for example in an article,
  a video, a mod list, a compatibility table or a link to stereopticon.org.
- Say that your product or mod works with wiz3D, Stereo Loom or Stereopticon, if it does.
- Use the logos in community fan material that is clearly not official.

## Please don't

- Use the names or logos as the name or logo of your own product, fork or service.
- Change the logos, or combine them with other marks.
- Suggest that the Stereopticon projects endorse, sponsor or are affiliated with you
  without permission.

For anything else, open an issue or get in touch at stereopticon.org.

If you fork the code, that's welcome under its licence; just give your fork its own name and logo.
