---
title: stereopticon.org is live
date: 2026-11-08
summary: A new home for wiz3D, Stereo Loom and the Stereopticon launcher, with a game compatibility list, stereo screenshots and a fix guide.
---
wiz3D, Stereo Loom and the Stereopticon launcher now live together under the [Stereopticon-Org](https://github.com/Stereopticon-Org) organisation on GitHub, and they have a website.

Here's what's on it:

- **[Game compatibility](#compatibility):** over 400 games, how well each one runs in stereo, which wiz3D folder to install, and known issues.
- **Stereo screenshots** on game pages, viewable in wiggle, cross-eyed, parallel, anaglyph or on a 3D TV.
- **[A fix guide](#fixes)** for finding and fixing a broken effect yourself with ShaderHunter and wiz3D's fix primitives.
- **[API support](#apis)** showing where each graphics API stands, including what's still to come.

Tested a game? Use **Report a result** on its page. Approved reports update the compatibility list, and your stereo screenshot can appear on the site.

New wiz3D, Stereo Loom and Stereopticon releases will show up here automatically.
